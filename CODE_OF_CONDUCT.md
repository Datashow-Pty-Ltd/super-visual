# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.
Contributors must communicate constructively, assume good intent while engaging
with evidence, respect privacy and consent, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, or abusive behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
